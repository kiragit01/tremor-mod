# Stage 4d REACH (SPEC 7.2, "Шелест"): leaves under the feet rustle, so a step on them is heard louder than on stone
# (hearing.rustlingFactor 1.5 against stone's 1.2), and the leaves the player rustles in do not damp the rustle on
# its way, while other leaves along the way still damp like an insulator. The entity waits 15 blocks south of a line
# the player walks along (ai off, so it stays put); /tremor debug hearing shows each step on the action bar (footing,
# average resistance of the way, perceived loudness). The same walk on: A. stone; B. a strip of leaves one block
# wide; C. a floor of leaves 9 blocks wide (B and C louder than A); D. stone with a band of leaves between the player
# and the entity (quieter than A). Uses the sky platform of stage3-aggression.txt (repaired here).
