# The stalactite crawler (1.4.0). A closed stone cave, 8 high, dark. A crawler on the ceiling 10 blocks off hears the
# player, creeps along the ceiling to over them, crackles and drops stalactites (the player is struck, knocked away);
# heard again it drops two, then three. Then a
# lantern beside it: it sinks into the rock. Then another one is killed by breaking its block. Then the natural spawn
# (chance 1). The copies are tinted red; night vision to see in the cave. The server log tells what each one did.
# Run: gradlew runClientAutotest -Ptremor_autotest_script=stalactite-crawler.txt

cmd time set noon
cmd tremor despawn all
