# while the entity seeks at the top of its anger is not found by chance. With nothing heard the seeking entity roams,
# and its roam must keep away from the player (behavior.minWanderDistance, as DORMANT does, and its way passes the
# player nowhere within awakening.reachDistance), so that no Awakening starts and after awakening.seekSeconds (35) it
# calms down to HUNTING. 1. The player hides in a closed stone hut and sees none of the roam targets (until

# the entity. 3. The player sprints (heard: the anger tops out), then gets quietly 14 blocks away from the noise and
# keeps still: the entity searches only awakening.searchRadius (4) around the last sound, so with its reach (8) it
# does not find the player. Uses the sky platform of stage3-aggression.txt (repaired here). Each part ends with
# "stage hunting" in /tremor info and "No Awakening here" from /tremor awaken stop (the server log has "reached
# nobody within 35 s" for each). The seeking roam of part 1 drifts away from the hut (53-75 blocks), so the wandering
# after a calm-down is checked close by: 1b. A new entity 26 blocks south of the hut seeks for 2 s only
# (awakening.seekSeconds), calms down to HUNTING and wanders on through ALERT and DORMANT for 60 s while the player
# stays hidden, with behavior.wanderKeepAway on: every roam target ("Target ... (roam)") at least
