# Stage 4d REACH (SPEC 8 AWAKENING, 9 phase 1): at the top of its anger the entity seeks a player and the Awakening
# starts only once its bump has reached one (awakening.reachDistance, 8 blocks). 1. A quiet player 35 blocks away is
# not reached: after awakening.seekSeconds (shortened to 15 here) the entity calms down to HUNTING (anger 80), then
# the anger decays. 2. A player sprinting on stone is heard: the entity comes, and within reach the build-up starts
# around the player. Uses the sky platform of stage3-aggression.txt (repaired here). The server log has the lines
# "has reached <player>" and "reached nobody within 15 s".
# Run: tools/gradle-locked.sh runClientAutotest -Ptremor_autotest_script=stage4d-reach-seeking.txt

waitchunks
cmd gamemode spectator

cmd gamerule doDaylightCycle false

cmd tremor restore
cmd tremor despawn
cmd kill @e[type=!player]
cmd tp @s 300.5 201.0 300.5 0 8
wait 40
waitchunks
