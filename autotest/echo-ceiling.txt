# The echo of a tremor on a ceiling (1.4.0, the user: found standing on the floor, though no ring passed under them).
# A cave room 6 blocks high; a tremor ALERT on its ceiling, the player on the floor under it, 4 blocks off. A stone
# lands by it: it freezes and its ring runs out over the ceiling (as drawn), so it finds nobody: no "its echo finds"
# in the log. Then the same tremor on the floor finds the player (the echo still works).

waitchunks
cmd gamemode spectator
cmd time set midnight
cmd tremor despawn all
cmd kill @e[type=!player]
cmd tp @s 860.5 215.0 300.5
wait 40
waitchunks
cmd fill 840 195 280 880 199 320 minecraft:stone

cmd tremor stage alert
cmd tremor anger 35
cmd gamemode survival
