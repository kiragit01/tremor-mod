# movement.cavesOnly: the tremor keeps to caves. A tunnel under the ground with a shaft up to the sky at its east end:
# a spawn on the surface fails, one in the tunnel works; it comes along the tunnel under a player heard on the surface
# but never into the shaft's light (the sky's light runs some 15 blocks into the tunnel), nor out of it.
# Run: gradlew runClientAutotest -Ptremor_autotest_script=caves-only.txt
waitchunks
cmd gamerule commandModificationBlockLimit 2147483647
cmd gamemode spectator
cmd time set noon
cmd tremor despawn all
cmd tremor awaken stop
cmd kill @e[type=!player]
cmd tp @s 860.5 210.0 300.5
wait 40
waitchunks
cmd fill 830 170 270 890 199 330 minecraft:stone
cmd fill 830 200 270 890 200 330 minecraft:grass_block

cmd tremor spawn 850 200 290

cmd tremor spawn 840 184 301
cmd tremor stage hunting
cmd tremor anger 85
wait 20

cmd tp @s 862.5 201.0 301.5
cmd gamemode survival
cmd tremor voice 0
wait 120
cmd tremor info
log --- the player over the tunnel near the shaft speaks: it comes along the tunnel to where the light starts, no further
