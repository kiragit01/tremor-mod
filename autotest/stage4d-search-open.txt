# Stage 4d SEARCH (SPEC 9, phase 2): the network of ways on open ground, and the closing that never shuts the way.
# Part 1, the flattened surface at the world spawn (as stage4a-hollow.txt): the player is under the sky, so the true
# way starts with a throat, a trench 4-8 blocks away that goes down into the ground at a walkable slope, with one or
# two decoy throats into dead ends; the node lies under the ground. Shown with /tremor hollow status and walk, a
# spectator view from above the copy, and the way walked step by step (the feet go down).
# Part 2, natural terrain a little away from the spawn: the same, then a fast closing (no grace, 3 blocks a second):
# once the hollow has closed down to its smallest radius the way is still walked to the node, step by step.
# Part 3, the surface of the sky platform: no ground under it at all; the status says honestly whether the node got
# under the ground there.
# The player enters with /tremor hollow enter and leaves with /tremor hollow leave (no outcome, the real world is not
# changed). Sinking is held off (the player stands still while the screenshots are taken).
# Run: gradlew runClientAutotest -Ptremor_autotest_script=stage4d-search-open.txt
