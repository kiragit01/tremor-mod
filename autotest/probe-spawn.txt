# The debug probe calls tremors up (1.4.0, the user: no typing). With no tremor near, a right click (luring) calls one
# up where the player looks; with one there it goes there instead; held with left Alt (the probe's add key) a left
# click calls up one more of the kind chosen (a tremor at first). The server answers above the hotbar.
# Run: gradlew runClientAutotest -Ptremor_autotest_script=probe-spawn.txt
waitchunks
cmd gamemode creative
cmd time set noon
cmd tremor despawn all
cmd tremor crawler clear
cmd tremor listener clear
cmd kill @e[type=!player]
cmd tp @s 860.5 215.0 300.5
wait 40
waitchunks
cmd fill 830 195 280 890 199 320 minecraft:dirt
cmd fill 830 200 280 890 200 320 minecraft:grass_block
cmd fill 830 201 280 890 230 320 minecraft:air
config ambush.enabled false
cmd item replace entity @s weapon.mainhand with tremor:debug_probe
cmd tp @s 860.5 201.0 300.5 90 40
wait 30
log --- a right click, no tremor near: one comes up there
hold use 1
wait 40
log --- a right click again: it goes there
cmd tp @s 860.5 201.0 300.5 120 50
wait 10
hold use 1
wait 30
log --- left Alt + a left click: one more
cmd tp @s 860.5 201.0 300.5 60 45
wait 10
hold probe_add+attack 2
wait 30
cmd tp @s 860.5 201.0 300.5 160 45
wait 10
hold probe_add+attack 2
wait 30
log --- Shift + a right click: the menu
hold sneak+use 1
wait 10
screenshot 01_menu
cmd tremor despawn all
config ambush.enabled true
quit
