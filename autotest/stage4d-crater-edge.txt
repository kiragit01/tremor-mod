# Stage 4d, CRATER (SPEC 9 "Побег", "Победа", 12): an escape through the edge of the hollow saves the player and the
# things, but the crater still opens at the swallow point (without items), and the player comes out outside its
# footprint (radius plus the wobble of the rim plus a margin) whatever the config; a victory leaves no crater.
# D1: swallowed at 560 201 300, the player is put 28 blocks west in the copy and walks out over its edge (the
#     footprint of the default crater: 15 + 4 = 19 blocks).
# D2: awakening.craterRadius 20, craterDepth 8; swallowed at 560 201 420, the edge is called with /tremor hollow
#     outcome edge where the player stands (the middle: inside the footprint, 25 + 4 = 29 blocks).
# E:  swallowed in a closed 1x2 tunnel of a stone block at 560 209 520, the player breaks the node (as
#     stage4c-victory.txt); the block is counted before and after (clone ... masked counts what is not air).
# Sites: ground in the sky away from the sky platform of the other scripts: D1 x 520..600, z 260..340 (deep core
# x 536..584, z 276..324, y 170..197); D2 x 515..605, z 375..465 (core x 532..588, z 392..448, y 186..197);
# E x 545..575, y 195..215, z 505..535.
# Run: gradlew runClientAutotest -Ptremor_autotest_script=stage4d-crater-edge.txt
