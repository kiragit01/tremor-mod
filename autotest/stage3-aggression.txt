# Stage 3 acceptance (SPEC 15): in survival the entity appears by itself far away, reacts to noise by stage, can be
# angered and calmed down by silence; transitions are audible, contact while HUNTING hurts and throws the player.
# Arena: a 3-block-thick stone platform of 161 x 161 blocks high in the sky (y 198..200), so that the entity has
# nowhere else to wander and is always in view; a one-block perch above it that the entity cannot reach.
# The player stands at the centre (300.5, 201, 300.5) and looks south (+z).
# Since stage 4 the top of the anger (AWAKENING) seeks a player, and an Awakening starts once the bump has reached
# one who can be taken: the strikes of part 4 top the anger out right next to the player. Parts 4 and 5 are therefore
# played in adventure mode, which an Awakening never takes (SPEC 9) while the entity hears, strikes and throws the
# player as in survival; the entity then seeks for awakening.seekSeconds (35 s) and calms down to HUNTING before the
# silence brings it down further. Earlier runs may have left an Awakening's crater in the platform and its rubble and
# caches under it (repaired below) and the pause of natural spawns after an Awakening (lifted below by the config:
# part 6 needs natural spawns).
# Run: tools/gradle-locked.sh runClientAutotest -Ptremor_autotest_script=stage3-aggression.txt
