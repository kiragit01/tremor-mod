# Stage 4d, CRATER (SPEC 9 "Поражение", 12): a defeat leaves a real crater at the swallow point. B: lethal (the
# default): the player stands still in the hollow, the soft ground pulls them in, they die ("поглотила земля"); the
# crater opens and the things lie in caches of rubble on its bottom, some on top, some buried (no item entities, they
# would despawn). The caches are checked one by one, one lying open is looked at closely (its darker top, a glint now
# and then), one is broken by hand and the others by a command, and the things are picked up and counted against the
# known set given before (the helmet with the curse of vanishing is gone, as in vanilla). C: the soft variant
# (awakening.lethal false): alive on the rubble of the bottom, weakened (1 health, the effects, and hungry: food 6, no
# saturation, so the health does not come back by itself: still 1 twenty seconds later), with everything; no caches.
# D: a defeat called off by /tremor restore while its crater is being dug (slowed down): the player is brought out at
# the swallow point, now over the pit, and let down onto the layer dug so far instead of falling (no damage).
# E: a lethal defeat whose player is killed (/kill) while its crater is still being dug: the things wait for the crater
# and go into its caches once it is dug (no loose items, which would despawn).
# Sites (blocks of ground in the sky, y 170..200, away from the sky platform of the other scripts):
# B x 436..484, z 376..424, swallow point 460 201 400; C x 436..484, z 476..524, swallow point 460 201 500;
# D x 436..484, z 576..624, swallow point 460 201 600; E x 436..484, z 876..924, swallow point 460 201 900.
# Run: gradlew runClientAutotest -Ptremor_autotest_script=stage4d-crater-defeat.txt
